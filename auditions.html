What to Expect

How it works

1 · Get the call

Audition announcements for each production go out via the theatre’s Linktree and our social channels (Instagram, Facebook, X). Check back here and at linktr.ee/garfieldtheatre for dates.

2 · Sign up for a slot

Choose a time slot that works for you (or submit virtually). For the spring musical, virtual submissions go directly to Music Director Blake Saunders and Director Natalie Gress.

3 · Perform for the team

Casting is done by the director, music director, and their assistants. No choreography is required — just your best song or monologue. The audition panel is on your side.

Beyond the Cast

Production & tech team

Not a performer? Garfield produces every aspect of its shows in-house.

Applications for production and tech-theatre teams — set construction, lighting design, sound, costuming, stage management, props, and PR — are also available through our Linktree. If you’ve never built a set or hung a light, that’s exactly what we’re here to teach.

Competitions

Washington State Thespian IEs

Students register for Individual Events (IEs) through a teacher — talk to Ms. Gress if you’re interested. Thespian IEs include monologue, set design, lighting design, and more, and feed into the fall THESPYS! festival and the spring Washington State Thespian Festival.

Student-run theatre at Garfield High School, Seattle Public Schools. Three mainstage shows a year, plus student-led showcases.
