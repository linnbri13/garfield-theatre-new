2026 Spring Musical

The Addams Family!

An original musical about love, family, honesty, and growing up

America’s darkest family comes to life in this original musical about love, family, honesty, and growing up. The Addams Family features everyone’s favorite creepy, kooky characters, an ensemble of ancestors, and the Beinekes from Ohio. Join us for this comical feast that embraces the wackiness in every family.

Directed by Natalie Gress · Music Directed by Blake Saunders
Assistant Directed by Nora Luloff · Assistant Music Directed by Ayar Newman
Choreographed by Belen Alvarez and Malcolm Doty

Wednesday Addams has grown up and fallen in love with a sweet young man from a respectable family. Gomez must keep the secret from Morticia — until the night they host a dinner for Wednesday’s “normal” boyfriend and his parents, and everything changes.

2025 Fall Play

Almost, Maine

by John Cariani

Welcome to Almost, Maine, a place that’s so far north, it’s almost not in the United States. It’s almost in Canada. And it’s not quite a town, because its residents never got around to getting organized. So it almost doesn’t exist. One cold, clear, winter night, as the northern lights hover in the star-filled sky above, the residents of Almost, Maine, find themselves falling in and out of love in unexpected and hilarious ways. Knees are bruised. Hearts are broken. But the bruises heal, and the hearts mend—almost—in this delightful midwinter night’s dream.

Directed by Natalie Gress and Neely Seams

Student-Led Programs

Open to all students, no experience required — this is where most Garfield theatre people started.

Dramatic Paws Showcases

Students direct, stage manage, design and produce 3+ showcases of student-created Theatre, Improv, Video, Music, and Spoken Word. Open to all students; no experience required. Club officers choose a theme for each Dramatic Paws, and any club member who wishes to direct may submit a script for review.

5 Plays, 5 Days

Five days from script selection to performance — a set of one-act plays specifically for freshmen who want to get involved with theatre at Garfield.

Improv Club

The student Improv Club meets every Friday in the blackbox theatre (Q120), guided by local improviser and teaching artist Ben Lidgus. The club performs independently and during Dramatic Paws Showcases.

Student-run theatre at Garfield High School, Seattle Public Schools. Three mainstage shows a year, plus student-led showcases.
