The people behind the curtain

Staff & Faculty

Meet the team

NG

Natalie Gress

Theatre Director & Dept. Head

Secondary Theatre & CTE teacher at Seattle Public Schools (since 2020) and head of the Garfield performing arts department. Previously an English teacher at Issaquah School District (2018–2020), Natalie started out as a student teacher at Garfield in 2017–18, mentored by teachers Stewart Hawk and Stephanie Taylor. Directs the spring musical and fall play, and leads Thespian registration.

Student Honor Society

Garfield Thespians — Troupe #5419

A member of the International Thespian Society (ITS), the honor society for high school theatre. Students earn Thespian points and are inducted at 10.

Run entirely by students

Students produce, direct, stage-manage, design, and perform every Thespian production. Rehearsals happen at lunch or after school, so you never have to miss a class (and most of us have never missed a class).

Fall — THESPYS!

A Thespian individual event festival (see wathespians.org) where students compete in IEs like monologue, set design, and lighting design.

Spring — Washington State Thespian Festival

A 2–3 day festival at the Seattle Convention Center with workshops, showcases, and scholarships. Spring-musical cast members are strongly encouraged to attend. Each year the troupe also choreographs a Lip-Sync Contest number for the state festival.

Parents & Community

STaGe — Supporters of Theatre at Garfield

Supporters of Theatre at Garfield (STaGe) is a volunteer board of current parents/guardians. We provide programmatic support, fundraising and financial support, and organize volunteers to support mainstage productions and overnight field trips.

STaGe works hard to keep Thespian trip costs reasonable, and funds students who need support so that money is never the reason a student can’t compete or perform. If you’d like to volunteer, donate, or sponsor a show, email info@garfieldstage.org.

Student-run theatre at Garfield High School, Seattle Public Schools. Three mainstage shows a year, plus student-led showcases.
