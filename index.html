What We Do

More than a play — a full production company

🎬

Mainstage Productions

Three major productions a year — a spring musical, a fall play, and a winter show. Students act, sing, and design the sets, lighting, costumes, sound, and promotions for each.

🎧

Student-Led Programs

Dramatic Paws Showcases, 5 Plays 5 Days, and a weekly student Improv club. Open to all students — no experience required.

🏆

Garfield Thespians — Troupe #5419

Our student-run Thespian honor society competes in festivals, workshops, and showcases through the International Thespian Society.

Our Supporters

Supporters of Theatre at Garfield (STaGe)

Supporters of Theatre at Garfield (STaGe) is a volunteer board of current parents/guardians. We provide programmatic support, fundraising and financial support, and organize volunteers to support mainstage productions and overnight field trips.

Want to get involved?

Act, sing, build sets, design lighting, sew costumes, stage manage, or promote our shows. If you love theatre, there’s a place for you at Garfield.
