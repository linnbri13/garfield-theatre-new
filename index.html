More than a play

Garfield High School Theatre has a long-standing reputation for outstanding musical productions. Besides acting and singing, students learn every aspect of production hands on, from set-building and lighting design to costuming and PR.

01

Mainstage Productions

Three major productions a year — a spring musical, a fall play, and a winter show. Students act, sing, and design the sets, lighting, costumes, sound, and promotions for each.

02

Student-Led Programs

Dramatic Paws Showcases, 5 Plays 5 Days, and a weekly student Improv club. Open to all students — no experience required.

03

Garfield Thespians — Troupe #5419

Our student-run Thespian honor society competes in festivals, workshops, and showcases through the International Thespian Society.

The Addams Family!

Directed by Natalie Gress · Music Directed by Blake Saunders

America’s darkest family comes to life in this original musical about love, family, honesty, and growing up. The Addams Family features everyone’s favorite creepy, kooky characters, an ensemble of ancestors, and the Beinekes from Ohio. Join us for this comical feast that embraces the wackiness in every family.

Supporters of Theatre at Garfield (STaGe)

Supporters of Theatre at Garfield (STaGe) is a volunteer board of current parents/guardians. We provide programmatic support, fundraising and financial support, and organize volunteers to support mainstage productions and overnight field trips.

Get involved

Act, sing, build sets, design lighting, sew costumes, stage manage, or promote our shows. Auditions and production-team applications open each season.
